Internet is needed only once to install. After that the app works offline.

Open the app

iPhone / iPad

Use Safari. If the link opened inside a messenger app (KakaoTalk, LINE, etc.), tap its menu and choose Open in Safari first.

  1. Open the app address in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button
    (bottom of the screen on iPhone, top on iPad).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Keep the name Barcode QR and tap Add.
  5. While still online, open Barcode QR from the Home Screen once. This saves the app for offline use.

The Home Screen app keeps its own copy, separate from Safari, so step 5 is required.

Android

Use Chrome. If the link opened inside a messenger app, tap its menu and choose Open in Chrome (or "Open in browser") first.

  1. Open the app address in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app.
    If you only see Add to Home screen, tap it and then choose Install.
  4. Tap Install to confirm.
  5. Open Barcode QR from the Home screen or app drawer.

Samsung Internet: tap ☰ menu → Add page to → Home screen.
Chrome may also show an "Install" banner at the bottom by itself; tapping it does the same thing.

Using the app

  1. Choose Deposit or Dispense. For Dispense, enter the amount.
  2. Tap Generate.
  3. Show the Barcode or QR Code tab to the scanner. The text under the code shows what it will do.

Updates

When a new version is published, open the app once while online, then close it completely and open it again.

Troubleshooting

No "Add to Home Screen" / "Install" optionThe page is open inside a messenger or other in-app browser. Open it in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
Blank screen or "no connection" when offlineThe app was never opened online after installing. Connect to the internet, open the app once, then try offline again.
Old screen after an updateOpen the app online, close it completely, and open it again.
Remove the appLong-press the icon, then choose Remove App (iPhone) or Uninstall (Android).
